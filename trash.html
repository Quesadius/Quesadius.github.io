How to Play Trash

  • Goal: Flip every face-down slot in your layout by placing matching ranks in Slots 1 through 10.
  • Chain Reactions: Start your turn by drawing from the Deck or Discard pile. Place a playable card into its numbered slot, pick up the face-down card underneath, and keep chaining!
  • Card Ranks: Ace goes in Slot 1, 2–10 match their slot number, Jacks are Wild (any face-down slot), and Queens & Kings are unplayable Trash.
  • End of Turn: When you hold an unplayable card (Q, K, above your current level, or a slot already revealed), discard it to pass the turn.
  • Shrinking Rounds: Winning a round drops your next round by 1 card (10 → 9 → … → 1). Clear your 1-card round to win the match!
