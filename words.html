How to play

Goal. Score the most points by building crossword-style words on the board with your 7 tiles.

Your turn. Drag (or tap-select, then tap a square) tiles onto the board to spell a word in one row or column. The first word must cover the center ★. Every later word must connect to what's on the board, and every string of letters it forms must be a valid word. Then press Play — you'll draw back up to 7 tiles.

Premium squares apply only when a newly placed tile covers them:

DL double letter · TL triple letter · DW double word · TW triple word

Bingo! Playing all 7 tiles in one turn earns a +35 bonus.

Blanks can stand for any letter (you pick when you place one) but score 0.

Swap or pass. Instead of playing, trade in tiles (while the bag has some) or pass. Six scoreless turns in a row (passes or swaps) end the game.

Game end. When the bag is empty and a player uses their last tile, they collect the value of the opponent's leftover tiles. If the game ends on passes, each player's leftover tiles are deducted instead. The higher total wins.

Dictionary: TWL06, the North American Scrabble tournament word list (includes QI, ZA, and friends).
